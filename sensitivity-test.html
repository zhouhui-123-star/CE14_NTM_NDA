📌 Planning Remarks & Basis of Assumptions

  1. CEDD Reference: As per CEDD's email dated 4 Feb 2025, the average flat size in the Dedicated Rehousing Estate (DRE) is 55 m² (adopted in the Base Case).
  2. PlanD Advice: PlanD advised on 26 May 2025 to provide 2 towers in the DRE site accommodating ~800 units, assuming a Non-Domestic Plot Ratio (NDPR) of 0.3 only for the DRE site as it is assumed no Social Welfare Facilities (SWF) are accommodated.
  3. Public Housing Plot Ratio Breakdown: For public housing developments with a total non-domestic plot ratio of 0.625, a non-domestic plot ratio of 0.3 is adopted for retail, while GFA equivalent to a plot ratio of 0.325 is adopted for the provision of social welfare facilities (which is exempted from GFA calculation).
Calculation Formulation:
• $\text{Domestic GFA } (\text{m}^2) = \text{Development Area } \times \text{Domestic PR} \times \text{Site Reduction Factor}$
• $\text{Non-domestic GFA } (\text{m}^2) = \text{Development Area } \times \text{Non-domestic PR}$
• $\text{No. of Flats} = \text{ROUND}(\text{Domestic GFA} / \text{Flat Size})$
• $\text{Population} = \text{ROUND}((\text{Domestic GFA} / \text{Flat Size}) \times \text{Persons per Flat})$
