Tier 01

Promote Initiatives Under Prevailing Policies / Practices

Capitalizing on territory-wide environmental mandates and statutory guidelines.

CAP2050 & Waste Blueprint 2035
♻️

Resource Recovery Facility

Sort and recycle/upcycle plastics, glass, metals, paper, and yard waste to reduce overall municipal disposal.

🎯 Up to a rate of 55% according to CAP2050
🔥

Waste-to-Energy Facilities

Turn waste into energy to lower carbon emissions and eliminate long-term reliance on landfills.

🎯 In accordance with Waste Blueprint 2035
🔌

Provision of EV Charging Facilities

Provide EV charging-enabling infrastructure and charging stations across designated parking lots.

🎯 EEB / EPD Guidelines & HKPSG Standards
❄️

New Generation Refrigerants

Adopt lower Global Warming Potential (GWP) refrigerants across cooling systems to minimise fugitive emissions.

🎯 Refrigerant GWP < 15
Tier 02

Implement Project Specific Measures for NTM NDA

Spatial planning and engineering interventions tailored for Ngau Tam Mei.

TOD & Passive Urban Design
❄️

District Cooling System (DCS)

Centralized chilled water distribution serving air-conditioned buildings.

Reduce 20% cooling energy
🚆

Railway as Backbone

Modal shift from road to rail, reducing vehicle-kilometre-travelled (VKT).

Within 500m radius catchment
🚶

15-minute Neighbourhood

Meet daily needs within walking/cycling distance to reduce VKT demand.

Active mobility network
💨

Breezeways

Adoption of natural ventilation design for thermal comfort.

Reduces electricity demand
💧

Use of Reclaimed Water

Low grade water in non-potable uses like flushing and irrigation.

0.141 vs 0.243 kgCO₂e/m³ fresh
🏢

Green Building

Adopt BEAM Plus as tool to control building grade with passive design.

BEAM Plus certified
☀️

Renewable Energy (RE)

Incorporate on-site solar PV and Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) across rooftops and facades in line with BEAM Plus standards.

Equal to 2% building energy
🌳

Urban Forestry

Compensate tree felling and plant new trees for active carbon sequestration.

Compensate tree felling
Tier 03

SGR Infrastructures and Strategies for Technology Advancement

Smart, Green and Resilient (SGR) future-proof construction and power methods.

Future Tech & Embodied Carbon
🏗️

Modular Integrated Construction (MiC)

Adopt MiC to shorten construction periods and lower on-site waste to reduce both construction and embodied carbon of buildings.

Reduces embodied & construction carbon
🕳️

Common Utility Trough (CUT)

Optimise utility distribution networks within integrated troughs to avoid GHG emissions from repeated road-opening maintenance works.

Minimises maintenance emissions
⚡

Direct Current (DC) Grid

Provide both AC and DC power distribution systems within buildings to avoid multiple unnecessary electrical power conversions.

Eliminates AC/DC conversion loss
